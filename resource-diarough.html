CASE STUDY

Diarough — RFID across five global locations.

← Resource Library

Diarough N.V., a DTC Sightholder, selected Irys to extend RFID beyond stock audit and into daily diamond operations across Mumbai, Antwerp, Hong Kong, New York and Bangkok.

50,000+SKUs at rollout
5global locations
~8 monthsproject implementation
5+ yearssuccessful operation at article publication

What the implementation covered

Inventory checks, issue and return tracking, invoice and dispatch verification, and portable search for missing products. Printers, scanners and RFID tunnels were interfaced with SAP so RFID could operate inside the existing business system.

Why it matters

The project demonstrates Irys RFID as an operational layer rather than a standalone stock-counting tool, connecting physical inventory movement with enterprise workflows.
