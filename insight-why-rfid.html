Why adopt RFID?

← Resource Library

RFID gives jewellery businesses a faster way to identify and account for many tagged items without relying on one-by-one manual barcode scanning.

Where the value appears

Inventory counting is the obvious starting point, but RFID can also support search, movement control, issue/return workflows and verification when it is integrated into the operating system of the business.
