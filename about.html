Company

RFID intelligence with a jewellery-first perspective.

Headquartered in Singapore, Irys is a pioneer in inventory management for diamond and jewellery businesses using next-generation auto-identification technologies.

Technology that looks beyond stock audit.

Backed by domain expertise, the Irys suite combines IoT technologies to support inventory visibility across jewellery and diamond operations. With offices in Singapore, UAE, Hong Kong, India and the USA, Irys has deployed RFID technology for hundreds of jewellery and diamond companies worldwide.

Global by design.

Our footprint brings technology, implementation and support closer to jewellery businesses across major international markets.
